# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Billing operators and Plaqad Super Admins configuring plans, prices, product access, entitlements, subscriptions, and customer billing. They work in a dense operational dashboard and need to publish accurate commercial terms without editing code-shaped metadata by hand.

## Product Purpose

FlexPrice is the billing control plane for usage-based, credit-based, fixed, and hybrid pricing. For Plaqad, it must let authorized operators create a plan once, attach its entitlements, target one or more Plaqad products, and explicitly control how that plan appears on public Website surfaces.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a dependable administrative instrument: familiar controls, direct language, and clear previews of consequential settings.

## Anti-references

- Raw key/value metadata as the primary workflow for common publishing tasks.
- Opaque settings whose effect is visible only after checking another system.
- Decorative consumer-style UI that weakens the dashboard's operational clarity.
- One-off controls that do not match the existing FlexPrice component vocabulary.

## Design Principles

- Make consequential state explicit: publication, product targeting, and entitlement coverage must be readable at a glance.
- Prefer structured controls and previews over memorized metadata keys.
- Separate presentation from enforcement: Website cards use the plan's ordered public highlights, while linked entitlements remain the complete operational contract.
- Keep advanced metadata available without making it the normal path.
- Show operators where a plan will appear before they publish it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operation, labels, validation, and status messaging. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on color alone to communicate publication or entitlement state.
